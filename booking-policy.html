Before your session

Booking policy

Please review the confirmation sent after you book. It contains the current payment, access and session-specific terms.

Booking and payment

Submitting a time through Calendly begins the booking process. Follow the payment and confirmation instructions you receive. A time should not be treated as final until it is confirmed.

Changes and cancellations

If your plans change, use the rescheduling or cancellation link in your confirmation as early as possible. Contact the Santuri team for the terms that apply to refunds, late changes or missed sessions.

Using the space

Use equipment responsibly, follow staff instructions and leave the room as you found it. Tell the team in advance about specialist equipment, accessibility or staffing requirements.

Questions

For the latest terms or help with an existing booking, email info@santuri.org.
