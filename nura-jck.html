Weekly CDJ practice · Santuri East Africa

NURA x JCK

Time behind the decks.

NURA x JCK runs regular CDJ practice sessions at Santuri in Nairobi. Join the Friday sessions in the DJ Practice Room or the Saturday sessions in the Classroom.

Plan your visit

Two days. Two spaces.

Both sessions run from 11:00–18:00, Nairobi time. Choose the day and space that work for you, then check the booking calendar for an available time.

Friday · 11:00–18:00 EAT

DJ Practice Room

NURA x JCK’s Friday CDJ practice sessions take place in Santuri’s dedicated DJ Practice Room.

Saturday · 11:00–18:00 EAT

Classroom

The Saturday sessions take place in Santuri’s Classroom.

Before you book

Make time to practise.

Sign in to choose an available time and send a booking request. Sessions are subject to availability and confirmation by the Santuri team.

Everyone is welcome at Santuri. Please use equipment responsibly and follow staff guidance. Read our booking policy and house rules before your visit.

Find us at Santuri East Africa, Basement, The Mall, Chiromo Road / Ring Road Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya.

For questions about the sessions, contact bookings@santuri.org.
