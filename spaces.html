Shared-space care

A few house rules.

These guidelines help keep the spaces safe, welcoming and ready for the next person.

  • Drinks and equipmentKeep liquids in closed containers and away from equipment, consoles and work surfaces.
  • Smoke-free indoorsSmoking and vaping are not permitted inside any Santuri space. Please ask staff where smoking is allowed.
  • Personal belongingsKeep valuables with you and check the room before leaving. Santuri cannot take responsibility for unattended personal items.
  • Respect and inclusionEveryone is welcome at Santuri. We ask all visitors to treat one another with dignity and respect; harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.
  • Care for the roomFollow staff guidance, use equipment responsibly and leave the space as you found it.
