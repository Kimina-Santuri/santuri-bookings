Read carefully

Operational rules.

These shared expectations help protect members, staff, equipment and everyone’s access.

Safeguarding
Everyone is welcome at Santuri. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or unsafe behaviour of any kind is not tolerated. Members must respect others, follow staff guidance and report safeguarding concerns to a Santuri team member.
Booking
All production and studio slots must be booked at least 24 hours in advance through the Community Manager. Sessions, spaces and equipment remain subject to availability and must be confirmed by the Santuri team.
Cancellation
Cancellations must be made at least 12 hours before the session. No-shows will forfeit their hours for that week.
Liability
Members are responsible for the proper care of equipment during their sessions.
Payment
Membership is active for 30 consecutive days from payment confirmation. Renew before the membership expires to maintain uninterrupted access; expired memberships automatically revert to the Free Tier.
