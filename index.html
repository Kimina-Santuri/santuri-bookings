Santuri Membership · for alumni

Time in the room, every week.

Monthly memberships give Santuri alumni weekly production and studio time, plus discounts on gear and space rental. Each runs for 30 days from payment confirmation.

Santuri Mwanzo

500 KES / month

  • 1 hour a week at the production station
  • Sound technician available
  • Standard gear and space rates

Santuri Midi

1,500 KES / month

  • Up to 4 hours a week at the production station
  • 2 × 2-hour pro studio sessions a week, with a technician
  • 30% off gear and space rental

Santuri Sana

3,000 KES / month

  • Up to 8 hours a week at the production station
  • 4 × 2-hour pro studio sessions, with a technician
  • 50% off gear and space rental

Weekly hours refresh every Monday and don’t carry over. Alumni can also stay connected on the Free Tier at no cost. Compare membership ↗
